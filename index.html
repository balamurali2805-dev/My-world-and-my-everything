Day and Night in the Theory

The sunflower theory feels a little different when you think about both the light we share and the moments when we have to become each other's light.

During the day

Sunflowers face and follow the sun together, representing good times when you both focus on growth, positivity, and external happiness.

At night

When the sun goes down and the light fades, the theory suggests the sunflowers turn to face each other, finding warmth and light in one another.
